# Stop Slop Writing Guidelines

Adopt the writing rules from `hardikpandya/stop-slop` for all user-facing copy, UI labels, error messages, and documentation:

1. **Cut filler phrases and buzzwords**:
   - No throat-clearing announcements ("Here's why", "The truth is", "Let me be clear").
   - No corporate jargon ("navigate", "unpack", "game-changer", "double down", "landscape").
   - Cut adverbs ("literally", "genuinely", "honestly", "fundamentally", "crucially").
   - Drop puffery ("Enterprise-grade", "State of the art", "High-clearance zone", "Pro").

2. **Use direct, active voice**:
   - Give human subjects real actions.
   - Avoid passive voice ("Permissions are verified" -> "The server verifies permissions").
   - No false agency ("The dashboard empowers users" -> "You can edit your profile from the dashboard").

3. **Be specific and factual**:
   - State facts directly without rhetorical buildup.
   - Avoid binary contrasts ("Not X, but Y", "It's not about X, it's Y").
   - Avoid dramatic sentence fragments.
   - Do not use em dashes.

4. **Trust the reader**:
   - Skip condescending prompts ("Think about it", "Let that sink in").
   - State what features do simply and accurately.
